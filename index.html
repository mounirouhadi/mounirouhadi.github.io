COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions worth answering before you decide

What is AI search visibility for suppliers?

When a procurement manager or brand founder opens ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Claude and asks for suppliers, the tool returns a shortlist. AI search visibility is whether your company shows up on that shortlist, how it is described, and whether that description reflects your real qualifications.

The suppliers that appear get considered before the first conversation even happens. The suppliers that do not appear are not necessarily rejected. They are simply never seen.

Why are many qualified suppliers invisible to AI tools?

AI tools rely on content they can find, read, understand, and cite online. Many qualified suppliers built their reputation through relationships, referrals, trade shows, repeat customers, and industry knowledge. That credibility often does not exist in a format AI tools can use.

Certifications may be buried in PDFs. Case studies may sit inside sales decks. Customer success stories may live only in private conversations. If the information is not public, structured, and easy to find, AI tools are unlikely to use it.

How do buyers actually search for suppliers in AI tools?

Buyers usually do not search by supplier name. They search by capability, certification, geography, industry, and use case.

A cosmetics brand might search: "GMP certified contract manufacturer ISO 22716 US East Coast." A supply chain director might search: "C-TPAT certified 3PL pharmaceutical cold chain Northeast." A medical device company might search: "ISO 13485 contract manufacturer FDA registered Class II."

AI tools build shortlists based on the suppliers whose public content clearly matches those terms. A supplier may have every relevant certification, but if those credentials are hidden in PDFs or missing from the website, the supplier may not appear.

Which certifications matter most for AI visibility?

The most important certifications are the ones buyers actually include in their search queries.

For manufacturing, this may include ISO 9001, AS9100, IATF 16949, ISO 13485, FDA registration, EU MDR, CE marking, UL certification, GMP, and ISO 22716.

For food and beverage, this may include SQF, FSSC 22000, ISO 22000, HACCP, FSMA, FDA food facility registration, and USDA.

For logistics and supply chain, this may include C-TPAT, AEO, TAPA FSR, ISO 28000, and EPA SmartWay.

Having the certification is not enough. The credential needs to be visible in clear, structured, public content that AI tools can read and connect to the right buyer searches.

How is AI visibility different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking in Google search results. AI visibility, sometimes called AEO or GEO, focuses on appearing inside AI-generated answers.

A supplier can rank well on Google and still be missing from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Claude responses. AI tools look for different signals when deciding which companies to mention, describe, or cite.

SEO still matters because brand-owned websites can feed AI visibility. But the goal is different. You are not only trying to win a blue link. You are trying to become part of the answer.

How long does it take to improve AI visibility?

Early citation signals on Perplexity may appear within 4 to 8 weeks after new content goes live. ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude usually take longer, often around 6 to 12 weeks.

Meaningful improvement across platforms usually takes 3 to 6 months of consistent execution. Timing depends on how quickly the supplier implements the content, how accessible the website is, and how active competitors are in the same category.

Suppliers that publish clear, structured, buyer-relevant content tend to move faster than suppliers that wait or only make small website updates.

Understanding the landscape

AI search goes by many names.
The problem is the same.

Whether your team calls it GEO, AEO, AI SEO, LLMO, or AI search optimisation — the underlying challenge is identical: ensuring your business is the answer AI tools give when buyers search for suppliers like you.

GEO
Generative Engine Optimisation

Structuring content so AI systems cite your brand in generated answers. SEO gets you found. GEO gets you trusted enough to be the answer.

AEO
AI tool Optimisation

Optimising for direct answers across AI platforms and voice search. Focuses on structured, extractable content that AI systems can cite precisely.

AI SEO
AI Search Optimisation

The broad practice of optimising online presence for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) rather than traditional keyword-ranked results pages.

LLMO
Large Language Model Optimisation

Ensuring that the large language models powering AI search tools recognise your brand as a credible, citable source, through structured content, external authority signals, and entity clarity.

AIO
AI Visibility Optimisation

A broader term covering all practices that improve how a brand or supplier appears across the full spectrum of AI-powered discovery tools, from search to recommendation engines.

This website is structured to be found by AI search engines across every terminology variant: GEO, AEO, AI SEO, LLMO. It uses structured FAQ schema, clear entity language, and buyer-query-aligned content.
